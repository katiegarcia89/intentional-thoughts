# Original Father’s Day card

The original `index.html` was copied byte-for-byte to `fathers-day.html` before the Halloween homepage was created on October 31, 2026. The original `styles.css`, `script.js`, and everything in `assets/` are unchanged.

The new homepage’s **Father’s Day ’26** tab loads that original page. It also offers a direct link to open the card in its own browser tab. The direct URL is `/fathers-day.html`; the integrated tab is `/#fathers-day`.

Halloween lives in `index.html`, `halloween.css`, and `halloween.js`. Its photographic-style horror artwork and realistic black cat are custom generated assets in `assets/halloween/`. The cover has a house silhouette; the six chapters include witches, skeletons, pumpkins, ghosts, werewolves, and a glowing Halloween finale. A canvas draws moving textured fog. Horror sounds are generated locally with Web Audio after the visitor enters or enables sound; the sound button mutes them. No build step or third-party sound files are required. Serve this folder as a static website or upload the whole folder to the existing static host. No deployment has been performed as part of this update.
